How we cook

The tins are the same as our
grandmothers'. The plates aren't.

Zestora started as an argument at a family dinner about whether a samosa really needs to look like a samosa. It does not. What it needs is the potato spiced right and the pastry to shatter when you bite it — everything else is negotiable.

We still buy whole spices from the same shop in the Paltan Bazaar our head chef's mother used. We just don't feel obligated to plate things the way she did.
